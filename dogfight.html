Air-combat maneuver dictionary

The AI chooses from these maneuvers according to geometry, energy, pilot experience, fatigue, and current survivable G. Values are deliberately game-scale—not medical or flight-training guidance.

Green pilots use fundamentals; Experienced pilots add vertical and rolling options; Aces have the full library and the highest G tolerance.
